On your iPhone

  1. Open this page in Safari. Tap an app's Install button and confirm the iOS installation prompt.
  2. Return to your Home Screen and wait for the app to finish installing before opening it. Repeat for the other apps.
  3. For the advertising path, select the adult age option only when appropriate. Demo ads appear at the app's designed placements once ownership and consent checks allow them.
  4. Check the no-ad paths, Remove Ads screen and Restore Purchases. Use a supported Apple test environment for transactions; do not make real purchases just to test.

Keep your existing app data. Do not delete a published app just to force installation. If installation fails, keep the original app and record the exact iOS error. Updating an existing paid app is important for checking historical ownership.

These packages are signed for the existing registered iPhone 16 Pro Max. Downloads are public, but other devices cannot install them without matching provisioning. Apple provisioning profiles embedded in the IPAs contain registered device identifiers. No TestFlight is required. Successful signing and available download links do not prove a successful physical-device installation or purchase.
